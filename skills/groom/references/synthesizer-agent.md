# Groom Synthesis Guidance

This optional reference supports `skills/groom/steps/04-synthesis.md`. The current phase and result contract come from the canonical session and `references/state-schema.md`; this document does not define a separate legacy route or persona-count requirement.

## Goal

Produce compatible, evidence-bound decisions about audience/JTBD, problem, alternatives, scope, risks and measurement while preserving disagreements and uncertainty.

## Execution

Use the actual available runtime capabilities. Answer cohesive questions inline or delegate distinct questions when independent reasoning helps. Provider names do not change product policy. A synthesizer owns the judgment needed to reconcile conflicting answers; it is not merely a mechanical merger.

- Read only relevant named evidence and approved context. Trace material claims to their original observation, source locator and date; file existence and citation counts do not prove support.
- Form candidate jobs from the audience's trigger, desired outcome and present workaround. Compare claim fit, authority, independence, recency and consequence. More citations do not make one JTBD dominant; several summaries of one observation remain one evidence chain.
- Keep a primary job only when the evidence and chosen scope support it. If competing jobs materially change the recommendation, explain that choice and use the smallest forcing question; otherwise retain the assumption and reversal condition.
- Reconcile dependencies before merging scope and risk answers. An in-scope item must not silently depend on an excluded capability. An exclusion can create a risk; do not remove risks merely because their concern appears in non-goals.
- Evaluate rare but severe failure states and domain invariants. Connect each risk to a scoped outcome, evidence or explicit hypothesis, with a mitigation or honest accepted limitation. Do not fabricate mitigations from nonexistent infrastructure or fill an arbitrary risk quota.
- Compare materially selectable alternatives, including the current workaround when credible. Show the sacrifice of each and what evidence would make the rejected option preferable.
- State what success could be observed, which baseline is unknown and how interpretation could change the choice. Never convert assumed impact into a measured result.

## Done-when

The structured synthesis explains its consequential derivations, contains compatible scope/dependencies, preserves contrary evidence, and gives downstream design/drafting no hidden product decision to infer. Record it through the current session runner. **Advance:** return to Step 4's completion contract, then proceed to Step 5 (Design).
