# Product decision to technical delivery

Approved scope: four focused continuations from v1.13.88. This does not redo shipped quality, RFC maintenance, QA recovery, OpenCode or first-slice composition work.

1. Retain an evidence-backed value recommendation in canonical Groom content and both readers. Cite evidence and commercial assumptions; keep unknown buyer and counterevidence limits explicit. Reuse current idea ranking.
2. Provide runnable in-app Groom mode alongside inert prototypes. Isolate exact app source and separate mock fixtures, observe required journeys, bind source/fixture/launch/state identity, then adopt only reviewed UI code at the exact Dev base. Preserve source until adoption.
3. At explicit product approval, optionally grant bounded technical derivation/implementation for the exact content. Independently reviewed initial RFC and maintenance retain that product identity; technical bytes remain reviewed. Product, commercial, security, privacy and operational changes or uncertainty require decisions. Platform permissions remain separate.
4. Continue scoped Design Critique/Review recovery past their former count stops with immutable history, grounded diagnosis, changed approach, fresh evidence and resource bounds. No fake pass, reset or unchanged retry loop.

## Pilot acceptance

`tests/product-autonomy-pilot.test.js` runs actual Groom approval, RFC artifact/audit/archive and Dev readiness contracts against an isolated incremental app fixture. It checks producer marker preservation, value presence/legacy readability, realistic app source/fixtures and state binding, an exact original delegation, injected privacy escalation, fixture drift and UI-only adoption. Lane tests add stale/replay/unsafe-path/forged-source/uncertain-review and post-threshold recovery cases; ordinary approval, historical sidecars and OpenCode profiles remain tested.

The separate manual browser pilot uses an incumbent shell, navigation, detail page, gallery and drawer. It adds explicit manual Save, enters via ordinary navigation, saves partial checkpoints/note while still in progress, leaves and returns, and checks 20-item content plus a 390px viewport. Captured pixels, exact source/fixture/launch/state identity and checked adoption are retained in local delivery evidence. These are generic synthetic scenarios, not customer validation or live backend certification. Manual Save is a valid approved behavior.

## Limits

Structural checks establish exact bindings and freshness. They do not establish market truth, claim entailment or independent reviewer judgment. The runnable identity needs preserved source; it is not a portable source bundle. The pilot does not certify an actual consuming app's backend, native OpenCode host behavior, all viewport sizes, platform permissions or measured commercial results. Consumer KB, tracker and shared installed cache are outside this release's mutation scope.
